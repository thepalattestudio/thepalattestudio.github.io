Meet Your Teacher

Priti Rane

With years of hands-on teaching experience, Priti Rane founded The Palette Studio to give every student — from curious five-year-olds to lifelong beginners — a space to explore colour, technique, and confidence at their own pace. Her classes blend gentle structure with plenty of room for imagination, so every canvas feels personal.

Priti believes art is best learned in small circles, not crowded classrooms — which is why every batch is capped at just three students. It means real feedback, real encouragement, and real progress you can see, class after class.

Our Courses

A medium for every artist

Five hands-on mediums, taught step by step, in batches small enough for every question to get answered.

Acrylic Canvas Painting

Explore layering, rich texture, and vibrant colour compositions on stretched canvas with versatile acrylics.

Watercolor Painting

Master fluid washes, wet-on-wet blending, transparency, and delicate lighting effects on watercolor paper.

Pencil Sketching

Master line, cross-hatching, proportion, and tonal shading — the foundation every strong artist builds on.

Oil Pastel

Bold, blendable colour loved for its rich creamy consistency, vibrant gradients, and textural depth.

One Stroke Painting

Learn the signature decorative technique of double-loading brushes to paint petals and leaves in a single stroke.
